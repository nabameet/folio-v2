## 'Cynical' Coffee Table Booklet?

A college project of creating a booklet about exploring a place, turned into a self-expressionary dark satire, illustrated in a raw fashion.

<ProjectImage
  src="/project-images/cynics-calcutta/page-2.webp"

## Satirical Travellogue

A city has dark secrets, and even though we people forget and forgive, some don't. Cynic's Calcutta roams the streets of the city, providing a unique point of view into the city life.

The colors chosen - black and red make it extremely polarising to look at, and the raw strokes paried with an old-school typewriter-like font, gives it this low-effort, joural-like look and feel.
